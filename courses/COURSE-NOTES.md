# COURSE NOTES — Cottonwood & Rockdale Municipal

**From:** the art/design AI · 24 Aug 2026
**Against:** COURSE-BRIEF.md
**Files:** `cottonwood.ts` (par 32), `rockdale.ts` (par 31)

Directions chosen from §6: **the offset green** (Cottonwood) and **the short
course** (Rockdale). Not the exposed course (it is Pine Hollow's own identity
sharpened — too close to "the same idea in different clothes") and not the
unfair major (flagged as easiest to do badly; happy to attempt it as a
follow-up once these two have been through the harness).

All subtraction uses the **finishing** ladder from §2 (277 / 255 / 208 / 170 /
133 / 105 / 94 / 70 / 58 / 55 / 35) and diagonal-corrected distances
(correction ≤ 2 yds everywhere). Every leave below was computed, not eyeballed.
Geometry claims ("on the line") were checked by computing the direct line's
lateral position at the hazard's down-coordinate against the ellipse extent.

## Assumptions the harness should confirm or kill

These are the places where the brief was ambiguous or silent. Holes marked (†)
are sensitive to the answer.

1. **§2's own example subtracts 265, but the ladder says Bomb finishes 277.**
   "A 265-yard drive leaves 177" on the 442 — that is carry, not finish. I
   designed on **finishes**. If the engine subtracts carry for leaves, every
   tee-shot leave below shifts by the run-out (+12 Bomb, +30 Stinger, +8 LI).
   († Cottonwood 2, 4, 5, 8; Rockdale 4, 5 — the gap positioning moves.)
2. **`length` read as the along-axis coordinate of the green centre**, with
   `greenSide` perpendicular to it — not the true tee-to-centre distance. If
   length is the hypotenuse instead, Cottonwood's holes are each 1–2 yds
   shorter along the axis; no hole changes character.
3. **Hazards judge the finishing position only** (flight passes over), except
   Stinger/Bump-and-Run's explicit no-carry rule. († Cottonwood 4 and 8 use
   "fly the trees / the boring card drowns" — if flight-through-trees is a
   thing, Cottonwood 4 needs its copse shrunk.)
4. **"Shortens to the pin"** = the card plays min(its distance, distance
   remaining). Rockdale's birdie economy (holes 1, 3, 6, 8) leans on this.
5. **Scatter half-widths are lateral around the aim line**; depth error is the
   ±14 aim jitter. If depth scatter is bigger, Rockdale's small greens get
   meaningfully harder (which might be fine — it is the short course's only
   defence — but the birdie rate should be re-read before judging it).
6. Lie multipliers applying to shortened wedges from rough near water: I
   assumed a Pitch from rough plays 70×0.9 = 63. The softlock scan below
   holds under that assumption.

# COTTONWOOD — par 32 (front 15 / back 17)

**Identity: the straight line is a lie.** Every green but the 7th sits off the
tee line (−35 to +30), so the direct line runs diagonally across the corridor
and what stands on the diagonal is unavoidable — the engine's dogleg (§6).
Pine Hollow asks *do you own the number*; Cottonwood asks *do you trust the
line*.

**Front-four spread carriers (§5):** hole 2 (EV split + card-dependent), hole
3 (hold-the-right-card birdie), hole 4 (aggressive/safe fork with genuinely
different outcomes). Hole 1 is the teaching opener. Not four grinds: 390 / 460
/ 185 / 350.

# ROCKDALE MUNICIPAL — par 31 (front 15 / back 16)

**Identity: par is easy; the cut does not care about par.** The §6 short
course. Everything is reachable with owned numbers; par is protected by
greens at radius 13–14 and hazards parked exactly where the good cards
finish. The field goes low, so the place-based cut sorts from the **top** —
the spread is between birdie and tap-in birdie, and between greed that pays
and greed that finds the range fence. This is the course that proves the
cut-by-place system at the low-scoring end.

**Front-four spread carriers (§5):** hole 1 (two roads to birdie, one passes
a fence), hole 2 (hold-the-right-card), hole 4 (fly-the-oak fork). Hole 3
breathes. 305 / 150 / 330 / 300 — nobody grinds, everybody chooses.

## What I could not check without the harness

- **Birdie rate at Rockdale.** The design intent is a field median around −2
  to −4 per round with real spread in the tails. If everyone converges on the
  same number, holes 2/6/7 need their greens at 13 → the technique-dependence
  is the spread mechanism, and it only works if naked clubs putt from the
  fringe rather than hole out.
- **Whether Cottonwood 2 + 4 double-punish the same profile** (both lean on
  the 183-ish gap). If the harness shows them correlated, hole 6's lay-up
  number (145) can move to 150 by shortening the hole 5 yds to decorrelate.
- **Stinger finishing in Cottonwood 4's copse**: intended as a visible trap
  the cone warns about (the cone will overlap drawn trees — P8 does the
  teaching). If it reads as a gotcha in play, shrink rDown 42 → 34 so only
  the right half of Stinger's cone clips it.
- Expect a third of this to be wrong, per the brief. The tables above say
  what was intended; the harness gets the vote.
