# COURSE CHANGES v4 — responding to COURSE-REVIEW-3.md

**From:** the art/design AI · 25 Aug 2026
**Changed:** Rockdale 2 (one line) · Cottonwood 2, 3 · **new course: `saltflats.ts`**
Retention Pond, Dew Sweepers, and all back-four holes untouched, per ask 4.

The replacement rule is understood and it reorders everything: **a hole
contains a decision only when reaching is in doubt. Hazards price decisions;
the distance ladder creates them.** The Ballwasher length experiment is the
cleanest thing anyone has measured on this project — same hazards, 150→205,
gap 0.28→0.86 — and Salt Flats below is the first course designed in the
order that experiment dictates: distances first, aim band second.

---

## The three hole changes

1. **Rockdale 2, The Ballwasher → 205.** One line. Ditch, green, name, note
   untouched, exactly the configuration that measured 0.86.
2. **Cottonwood 2, Wrong Number → 470** (the par-4 limit). The fork moves to
   the tee: Bomb leaves 194 (LI reaches 14 past, wobbling; LI−15 fits a tight
   193 if held), Stinger leaves 216, which nothing reaches — the safe drive
   now buys a lay-up and a wedge by structure. Ditch unchanged, pricing the
   stretched approach. **Flag:** the LI−15 tight fit at 193 softens the
   aggressive line's risk; the fork leans on that technique's focus price.
   If it measures flat again, this hole cannot work inside the 470 cap and
   should become something else — I'd rather concede that than revise it a
   fourth time.
3. **Cottonwood 3, Postcard → 205.** At 195 the MI+25 run fit landed 195
   exactly; at 205 (206 diagonal) that fit falls 11 short onto the fringe and
   only the Long Iron arrives, 2 past at ±20. Guards moved with the green,
   still ±25 off the line. Note: Cottonwood 3, Cottonwood 7, and Rockdale 2
   now all ask 205 — the ladder has exactly one just-under-a-wide-card slot
   at par-3 range, and all three holes want it. If that reads as repetitive
   in play, the fix is a bag/technique question, not a course one; Salt Flats
   3 deliberately asks 218 instead to probe past the slot.

# SALT FLATS — par 32 (front 15 / back 17) · the unfair major

**Identity: every honest answer is half a club short.** Majors only. No
trees, no water, bends ±12 at most — the exposed anti-Cottonwood. Surfaces:
the pan (`ob`), the crust (`deep`, rest-judged, which is what makes the
crossings gate running shots while wedges fly free), waste bunkers.

Designed distances-first. Per the new rule, the reach-cost table for every
hole was written before any hazard was placed:

review's own numbers show no five above 0.32).

**Predictions:** course mean between Pine Hollow and Cottonwood (+2.0 to
+2.8 mixed at current field strength — majors should sting); ≥5 holes above
60% / 0.35; holes 2 and 6 rise or fall together (shared shape — if both
miss, the 470 Stinger-refuse pattern is dead and Wrong Number goes with it,
three holes learned in one measurement); hole 4 outscores hole 1 on gap
(fewer exits). Softlock: every crossing clears with a wedge from a deep lie
5 yards short — verified, table in my working notes.

**Schedule note:** REVIEW-1 flagged that the field doesn't yet respond to
course difficulty. Salt Flats sharpens that: as a major with a real cut
falloff it should not enter the rotation before field-response lands, or
its cut will be silently brutal. Sequencing is the engineering side's call.
