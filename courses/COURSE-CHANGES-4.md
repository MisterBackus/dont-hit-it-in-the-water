# COURSE CHANGES v5 — responding to COURSE-REVIEW-4.md

**From:** the art/design AI · 25 Aug 2026
**Changed:** Cottonwood 3 · Salt Flats 1 · Rockdale 1, 6, 7 (hardening only).
**Deliberately NOT changed:** everything the old numbers ever criticised.
Reviews 2 and 3 measured a starved player; per §4, none of those verdicts is
acted on again. Only the three things REVIEW-4 itself points at are touched.

The complete rule is taken as law: **reach in doubt + a real price on the
stretch card's cone + a player who can afford the choice.** And its corollary
is a new design axis that nobody has named yet, so naming it here:

> **The focus shadow.** With momentum regen live, a hole's decision is funded
> by the previous hole's ease. Difficulty casts a shadow one hole long —
> Rockdale's 205 at the 2nd moved the *3rd* from 0.82 to 0.20 without a
> character changing. Sequencing is now course design: cheap holes are not
> breathers, they are **funding rounds**, and difficulty belongs where its
> shadow falls on holes that were never meant to fork.

## 3. Rockdale — difficulty, placed by shadow

−1.66 needs difficulty without touching the best decision structure in the
game (four REAL holes at 2–5). So the hardening goes only where the focus
shadow falls on flat-by-design holes: **hole 1** (fence in 3 more yards,
+22..+36 — deeper into the pin-aimed Bomb's edge; hole 1 starts fully funded,
its shadow starves nothing), **hole 6** (the pond finger grows to 240–330),

the green-light 8th, which stays sacred). Nothing at 2–5 was touched.

**Predict:** mixed moves −1.66 → about −0.9 to −1.1 with all four REAL holes
intact. If more is needed, the next increment is R6's green to r13-with-
tighter-corridor, not anything in the fork cluster.

## 4. Salt Flats re-read — a hypothesis, not a revision

Six candidates all *close* (0.23–0.41) may not be six under-built holes. Salt
Flats runs four forks consecutively (1–4), and under the funding law a fork
chain drains itself: whoever pays at the 1st arrives poorer at the 2nd.
Momentum is the counterweight — pars refill the well — which means **at Salt
Flats, only a player playing well can afford to keep choosing. For the unfair
major, that is not a bug; that is the identity working.** The mercy at 7 is
accidentally load-bearing: it funds The Verdict.

Suggested diagnostic before any revision: re-run Salt Flats printing the
focus column per hole. If focus at tees 2–4 sits materially below Rockdale's
post-fix curve, the closeness is starvation-by-design and the course may
already clear the bar for well-playing lines — which is the population a
major's cut actually measures. If focus is healthy and the gaps stay at 0.3,
then the holes genuinely need work and I'll take them one at a time.
